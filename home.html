Course structure

A pathway from foundations
to clinical practice.

The published course outline moves through four academic stages followed by a rotating internship.

  1. Year 1

    Foundations

    Study anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, and the basic sciences that support clinical learning.

  2. Year 2

    Systems and disease

    Explore pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, and how illness affects the body.

  3. Year 3

    Community and allied subjects

    Develop a wider understanding of health, communities, and related clinical specialties.

  4. Years 4-4.5

    Clinical subjects

    Bring medical knowledge together across core specialties and patient assessment.

  5. 1 year

    Rotating internship

    Apply learning through supervised rotations across clinical departments.

Course structure and duration are based on the published Meraki MBBS overview. Confirm current admission requirements and regulations with the official admissions team.
